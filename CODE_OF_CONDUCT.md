# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **IGNITE SPORTS CLUB — EST. 2026** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy collegiate community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our club include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors and student athletes
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and athletic/technical backgrounds
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall sports club and its members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as physical or email addresses, without their explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a collegiate sports or professional engineering setting

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including the project repository, pull requests, issue trackers, Discord/communication channels, and any representation of the club in online or offline sports activities.

---

## Contact & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the IGNITE Sports Club maintainers at `sportsclub@campus.edu`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
